# Hello, Farmer

Welcome to skills-for-agriculture — decision-making and guidance skills for farmers, growers, and land stewards.

If a name was provided, greet them by name. Otherwise greet them generically.

Briefly explain what this plugin does: it gives you structured thinking tools for agricultural decisions — from soil health and water management to transitioning to regenerative practices, permaculture design, livestock management, and more.

Ask what they're working on or what decision they're facing today.
